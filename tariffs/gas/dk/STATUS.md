# Denmark: gas tariff status

- Coverage: partial. Household and small business gas products of every supplier, from the regulator's gas price portal, for a yearly use up to 6,000 m3. Each plan is a whole bill including VAT: the unit rate holds the gas price, the distribution tariff, the emergency supply tariff and the gas taxes, and the standing charge holds the distributor's system tariff, the meter charge and the supplier's fees. Most products have a price that changes each month, and a plan holds the price listed on the day it was read. Introductory offers and spot products are not held.
