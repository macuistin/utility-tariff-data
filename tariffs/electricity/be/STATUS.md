# Belgium: electricity tariff status

[All countries](../STATUS.md)

- Coverage: partial. Only the fixed-price single-rate plans of Mega, TotalEnergies, Eneco and Elegant are held, from their monthly tariff cards. A bill also has network costs, levies and the green-power contribution, and a product may carry a sign-up discount, none of which are in these plans, so a bill priced from them is below a real bill. The one exception is TotalEnergies, whose card price already includes the green-power contribution. Indexed and dynamic products are not held.
