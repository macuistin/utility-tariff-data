- Coverage: partial. The fixed-price household electricity offers of Octopus Energy Italia, from its tariff page. The prices are the supplier's part only: the energy price and the fixed fee, before VAT and excise, with network losses. Transport and meter charges, system charges and taxes are not in them, so a bill is lower than a real one. The ARERA Portale Offerte, which lists every offer, refuses automated requests.
